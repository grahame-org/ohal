### 17.3.8 COMP power and speed modes

COMPx power consumption versus propagation delay can be adjusted to have the optimum
trade-off for a given application.

The PWRMODE[1:0] bitfileds of the COMPx*CSR registers allow setting the comparators to
high speed with full power or medium speed with medium power. Refer to \_Section 17.6: COMP registers*.
