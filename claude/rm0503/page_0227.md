### 7.3.1 General-purpose I/O (GPIO)

During and just after reset, the alternate functions are not active and most of the I/O ports
are configured in analog mode.

The debug pins are in AF pull-up/pull-down after reset:

- PA14: SWCLK in pull-down.
- PA13: SWDIO in pull-up.

PF3/BOOT0 is in input mode during the reset until at least the end of the option byte loading
phase. See _Section 7.3.16: Using PF3 as GPIO_.
