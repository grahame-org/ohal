### 15.4.2 DAC pins and internal signals

The DAC includes:

- One output channel
- The DACx_OUT1 can be disconnected from the output pin and used as an ordinary
  GPIO
- The dac_outx can use an internal pin connection to on-chip peripherals such as
  comparator, operational amplifier and ADC (if available).
- DAC output channel buffered or non buffered
- Sample and hold block and registers operational in Stop mode, using the LSI clock
  source (dac_hold_ck) for static conversion.

The DAC includes one output channel. The output channel can be connected to on-chip
peripherals such as comparator, operational amplifier and ADC (if available). In this case,
the DAC output channel can be disconnected from the DACx_OUT1 output pin and the
corresponding GPIO can be used for another purpose.

The DAC output can be buffered or not. The sample and hold block and its associated
registers can run in Stop mode using the LSI clock source (dac_hold_ck).
