#### Bit 13 USBSMEN: USB clock enable during Sleep mode

Set and cleared by software.
0: Disable
1: Enable
_Note:_ _This bit is significant on devices integrating the corresponding peripheral, otherwise it is_
_reserved. Refer to **Section 1.5: Availability of peripherals**._

1: Enable

1: Enable
