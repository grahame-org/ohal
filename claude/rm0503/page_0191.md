#### Bit 4 GPIOEEN: I/O port E clock enable

This bit is set and cleared by software.
0: Disable
1: Enable
Note: This bit is significant on devices integrating the corresponding peripheral, otherwise it is
reserved. Refer to Section 1.5: Availability of peripherals.

0: Disable
1: Enable
Note: This bit is significant on devices integrating the corresponding peripheral, otherwise it is
reserved. Refer to Section 1.5: Availability of peripherals.
